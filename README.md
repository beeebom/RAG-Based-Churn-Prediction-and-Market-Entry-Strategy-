## Overview

This project presents a comprehensive strategic analysis of Air India's turnaround journey — from decades of state-run losses and a forced merger to a new era under Tata Group ownership. It covers market dynamics, root causes of decline, and a structured growth roadmap.

## Key Findings

- **Market Gap:** Low-Cost Carriers (LCCs) control ~80% of domestic demand; IndiGo holds ~60% alone — leaving premium full-service routes underserved
- **Legacy Debt:** Air India carried ~₹46,000 Cr in debt and ₹69,570 Cr in accumulated losses at the time of acquisition
- **Revenue Base:** ₹19,815 Cr in FY2022; On-Time Performance improved from 6th to 3rd rank nationally
- **Merger Misstep:** The ill-timed 2007 forced merger of Air India and Indian Airlines compounded financial strain on both already-struggling entities
- **Dual-Brand Play:** Tata Group's consolidation strategy - Air India + Vistara (full-service) and AI Express + AirAsia India (low-cost) - targets both premium and budget segments

## Strategic Pillars (Tata Era)

1. **Fleet Optimization** - Reactivating idle aircraft to drive immediate revenue
2. **Digital & AI Transformation** - Automated systems for cost and scheduling efficiency
3. **Fixed Cost Scale** - Spreading overhead across a larger active fleet
4. **Synergistic Reorganization** - Unified management across full-service and budget arms
